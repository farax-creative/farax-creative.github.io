Changelog

What changed in each release of Zap Viewer. Updates are free for everyone who owns it.

2.10.0 Latest

Find a render in a long history, and see which settings changed between two of them. Everything new is off until you use it, so an existing history looks exactly as it did.

Added

  • Search the history by label or file name.
  • Filter to stills or sequences, and star renders to show only those.
  • Sort by newest, oldest, label or longest render. The menu opens on Default, which reorders nothing.
  • Render settings in the Info tab: engine, samples, denoising, resolution, camera, view layer, frame range, output format and colour management, recorded with every new render.
  • A/B compare lists what changed between the two renders, such as Samples 128 → 256. Nothing is listed when they were rendered the same way.

Fixed

  • A stereo (Multi-View) still that cannot be saved no longer leaves a pair of per-eye files behind in the history folder.

Corrected

  • Earlier notes said saved multilayer EXR stills were flattened. They never were: the history copy keeps every pass and view layer, channel for channel. If you avoided multilayer output because of that note, you did not need to.

Known limits

  • With Multi-View on, an F12 still is not added to the history, and the panel says so. Stereo sequences are kept.

2.9.0

Control over the disk space the history takes.

Added

  • Choose where renders are kept.
  • Point at animation frames Blender already wrote instead of copying them.
  • Let old test renders expire after a number of days you choose, or never. Locked renders are never touched.
