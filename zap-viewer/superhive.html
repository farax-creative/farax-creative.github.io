09Questions

Questions people ask first

Does it keep my EXR passes?

Yes. A saved multilayer EXR still is channel-for-channel identical to what Blender’s own renderer writes: Combined, Depth, Mist, Normal, Cryptomatte, every view layer. Earlier versions of this page said otherwise. That was wrong, and it never lost a pass.

Will my history change after the update?

No. Search, filters, stars and sorting are all off until you touch them. Renders made before 2.10 simply have no settings to show in the Info tab.

What about render farms?

In background renders (blender -b) it opens no window. Import the output folder afterwards.
