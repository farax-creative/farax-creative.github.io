Zap Board

Your references, inside Blender. A board that lives in your .blend — drop images in, arrange and mark them up, then keep working without leaving the window.

The problem

Your reference lives in another window.

It sits in a browser tab or on a second monitor, so every glance is a context switch — and the file you ship has no memory that the reference was ever there.

Zap Board puts the board on the same canvas as the work and saves it inside the .blend, so it opens with the scene on any machine.

The board

Drop it. Arrange it. Keep working.

Drag images onto the board and move them around like anything else on the canvas. It behaves like the editor you already know, not a modal dialog you have to fight.

Arrange

One key tidies the whole board.

Let a pile of images fall into a clean grid, or align and space them by hand. The layout is yours to nudge at any point.

Move

Grab many at once.

Box-select a cluster and move, scale or align it as one. The whole board stays under your hands, at the speed you expect from Blender.

Mark up

Draw straight on the board.

Arrows, boxes and notes to call out exactly what matters — in the colours you pick, right where the reference is.

Compare

Read your work against the reference.

Lay a card over your render, drop its opacity, and check the match — the oldest trick in the book, without ever leaving Blender.

  • Palette & picker. Pull the dominant colours off any image as swatch cards, or pick a colour straight into what you are shading.

Questions

Questions people ask first

Where do the boards live?

Inside your .blend file — images, notes and frames together. Open it on another machine and the board is still there, and you can keep several boards in one file.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free is a complete reference board. Pro adds the colour tools, image adjust, video and GIF, layout templates, send-to-3D and board export — a one-time $5 unlock in this same listing.

Does it work on Mac and Linux?

Yes, all three platforms. The only exception is Reveal File, which opens the folder on Windows only.
