Changelog

What changed in each release of Prompt Motion. Updates are free for everyone who owns it.

1.0.0 First release

Type what happens, and Prompt Motion generates the motion and keys it onto the armature you already have. Everything runs on your own machine.

Added

  • Text to motion: generate a clip from a typed prompt and bake it onto the selected armature as an ordinary Action.
  • Key poses: pose the first and last frame (or any frame with Add Key Here) and the generated motion passes through them.
  • Retargeting: bone matching by name and by skeleton structure, with presets for Mixamo, VRoid, MMD, Rigify, CloudRig, Auto-Rig Pro and DAZ Genesis 8 and 9. Every row is editable and saveable.
  • Hands: fingers posed from the rig's own finger bones, left and right independently, with the thumb solved onto the fingers.
  • Paths and goals: walk a drawn curve at a chosen pace, or Plan Route to a goal around marked obstacles.
  • Library: ten ready-made motions that apply with no server, GPU or download. Generated clips are saved to the same Library.
  • Prompts in other languages: Korean, Chinese and more, translated by a local model, with a built-in dictionary until it is downloaded.
  • One-button runtime install: sets up Python and fetches the model. No account and no token.

Safety

  • Refuses to bake and names the missing bones when the mapping cannot cover the joints the engine needs.
  • Warns before generating when a Rigify or CloudRig limb is left on IK, with one button to switch it.
